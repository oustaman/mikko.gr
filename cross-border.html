Cross-border systems

Regulated information,
with its evidence attached.

Consular rules, market closures, statutory deadlines, official statistics. Information scattered across jurisdictions by default, where a wrong answer costs money or a missed flight.

These are governance problems that arrive wearing an interface. One commitment across all of them: every fact carries its source, and an estimate is returned as an estimate rather than as a confident wrong answer.

  • timesys.pro — holidays, market closures and deadlines as rules rather than date lists. 241 countries, 57 exchanges, 36 calendar systems. Live.
  • orbisdata.org — a field guide to the world's official statistics. In build.
