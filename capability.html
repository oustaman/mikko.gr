05 · Capability

Capability,
not curriculum.

Designing the ability of an organisation to do something it could not do before — built as a system, delivered against employer requirements, and measured on what people can subsequently do.

RecognitionDigital Apprenticeship Provider of the Year — AAC Apprenticeship Awards, 2023
Scale25+ modules designed across undergraduate and postgraduate provision, including the integration of generative AI and immersive technology into studio practice
Digital deliveryDepartmental lead for digital delivery transformation — assessment frameworks, quality processes and staff development in educational technology
Employer-facingIndustry partnership frameworks, placement pipelines and live client projects; degree-apprenticeship provision delivered against employer standards
Research supervisionDoctoral supervision in the UK and at San Jose State University, California
Funded workKnowledge-exchange consultancy project leading a team of six academics

The distinction

Training transfers information. Capability changes what an organisation can do when nobody is watching — which means it has to be designed as a system, with its own decision rules, its own evidence, and an honest account of what it does not cover.
