Market entry

Products cross borders.
Interfaces don't.

Information density is a functional response to conditions, not a cultural preference. It does not survive the journey between markets equally in both directions.

A clarified product entering a dense market is verbose. A compressed product entering a clarified market is suspicious. Verbose is a criticism; suspicious is a verdict — and it is a judgement about trust, not a failure to understand.

Since June 2025 the European Accessibility Act has bound anyone placing digital products on the EU market, wherever they are established. Its technical requirements and the commercial fix are the same work.
