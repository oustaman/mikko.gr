The thesis

Decision Architecture

Choice architecture, from Thaler and Sunstein, taught institutions that a default is never neutral. Banks staff nudge units on exactly that premise. What that tradition theorises, a designer builds.

Economists describe the choice environment. Product managers brief it. I make it — the screen, the structure and the scent through which a real person moves toward a decision. That is ground a designer with a doctorate can hold that neither the economist nor the product manager can: the rigour to reason about decisions, and the craft to construct the thing that shapes them.

A search result is a claim about what matters. An information structure is a position on what gets seen.

Why it isn't a flourish

A coined title earns its keep when it is anchored to a real lineage and demonstrated in built work. The lineage sits where information architecture meets behavioural economics — Thaler and Sunstein on defaults, Tufte on the ethics of display, Norman on affordance. The built work is on the four pages that follow.

The through-line

Relocation intelligence, temporal data, jurisdictions, scripts, libraries, assessment. Different subjects, one practice. Each is an environment in which somebody has to decide something with incomplete information and real consequences.
