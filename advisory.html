Advisory

A mind to retain.

Some problems do not need another hire. They need someone in the room for a while who can see the decision underneath the design question, say the unwelcome thing, and leave the team better at thinking.

Where I am useful

Western-market readiness
An assessment of a product, its interface and its disclosures against European and Anglophone expectations and the European Accessibility Act, with a prioritised remediation plan.
Decision environments
Where a structure is producing the wrong choices, or producing the right ones in a way nobody can defend afterwards.
Provenance and regulated information
Systems whose output someone else's decision will rest on, and which therefore have to carry their own evidence.
Capability
Building an organisation's ability to keep doing this once I have left.

How it works

Short and defined: an assessment with a written argument at the end, or a standing seat for a fixed period. Not a retainer that quietly becomes staffing, and not a deck that describes a problem you already knew you had.
