Knowledge systems

Shared language,
or shared confusion.

Teams disagree less often than they think. More often they are using one word for several things and have not noticed.

A controlled vocabulary is not documentation. It is the precondition for deciding anything together — and it decays unless something enforces the standard.

  • UX Dictionary — a controlled taxonomy of roughly 14,500 terms
  • Definition Quality Scorer — clarity scored objectively, not to an editor's taste
  • hanzi.design — Chinese characters read as design rather than language
  • Naisu Press — digital editions with a stated method
