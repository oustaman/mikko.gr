Executive briefing · Market entry

One bank.
Two arguments.

HSBC runs six top-level categories in the United Kingdom and six in Hong Kong. Four are identical. The other two reveal two completely different theories of what a customer is.

Independent analysis · not commissioned by HSBC
Public retail websites, as observed July 2026
Mike Oustamanolakis · mikko.gr

01 · The surface

Identical at the top.
Not underneath.

Investing occupies 11% of UK navigation space against 17% in Hong Kong — a 55% relative increase.

02 · The finding

Four slots are fixed.
Two are a choice.

Banking, borrowing, investing and insurance are set by the business. The remaining two are discretionary — and the choice is the result.

Welfare and utility

Hong Kong — 19%

  • Offers

Market intelligence and commercial opportunity

03 · The absence

What isn't there
says more.

Under UK Wellbeing

  • Bereavement support
  • Losing your job
  • Mental health
  • Financial and domestic abuse
  • Support for gambling

Under HK Insights

  • Fund and bond screeners
  • FX outlooks
  • House views
  • ESG analysis
  • Stock quotes

There is no equivalent of bereavement support, job loss or mental health anywhere in the Hong Kong navigation. Not buried — absent.

And the UK has no stock screener, no IPOs, no warrants, no live market data.

04 · Reading the structure

Every slot is a claim
about who you serve.

  • Wellbeing at top levelUsers in difficulty are a primary case, not an edge case.
  • Insights at top levelUsers consume market intelligence routinely and expect direct access.
  • Offers at top levelUsers are motivated by incentive and go looking for it.
  • Five apps, not oneFinancial life is modular, and too varied for a single door.

05 · The correction

Neither one
is a mistake.

The UK structure is consistent with a regulatory environment that treats consumer protection as a duty. The Hong Kong structure is consistent with mass-market equity participation, where investing is an everyday behaviour rather than a specialist product.

Both are correct for the market they were grown in. The error is never the structure — it is moving one structure to a market it was not grown for.

06 · The implication

Information architecture
is not portable.

A navigation label is a promise. When it breaks, the user leaves — not because the content is missing, but because the path was built for someone else.

A company expanding across markets that ships its home structure unchanged is making a claim about its new customers it has never checked. This is why a product that performs at home can fail abroad without a single line of content being wrong.

The Hong Kong arrangement also carries a cost the UK's does not: a customer must know which category their app belongs to before they can find it. That assumes a product literacy the UK design never asks for.
